## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 1. RouteSense-AI — Connected Mobility Intelligence for a Smarter Chennai

Built as **Team Innovex** for the Urban Mobility Challenge (24-hour hackathon). The platform analyses Chennai Metro, MTC bus, suburban rail, road-network and bus-stop datasets to find **multimodal transfer hubs and connectivity gaps**.

- Technologies: React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, FastAPI, MySQL, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, Leaflet
- Worked with real datasets provided by a government organization.

Built at **Hack Summit 7.0**. It reviews a payment and gives a clear decision: **Allow, Verify or Block**.
